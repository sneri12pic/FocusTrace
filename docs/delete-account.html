FocusTrace — Delete your account

This page explains how to delete a FocusTrace cloud sync account and the data stored with it. FocusTrace is developed by Stepan Demianenko. An account exists only if you created one for optional cloud sync; without one, nothing is stored on a server and there is nothing to delete here.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open FocusTrace and go to Settings.
  2. In Account & sync, sign in if you are not signed in.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your current password and tap Delete permanently.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What is deleted on the server

  • Your account: the email address and the password hash.
  • Every sign-in session for the account, on every device.
  • Every device registered to the account (device name and installation identifier).
  • All synced usage data: the daily per-app totals uploaded from every device.

Nothing from the account is kept in the server database afterwards, and no copy is retained for later restoration. Server security logs may keep an internal account identifier for the deletion event; they contain no email address and no usage data. The email address can be used to create a new, unrelated account.

What stays on your device

Deleting the account does not change anything stored on your phone: usage history, restrictions, schedules and settings are kept, and sync is turned off. To erase the local data as well, use Settings → Clear local data or uninstall the app.

If you no longer have the app

Reinstalling FocusTrace, signing in and following the steps above is the quickest way. If you cannot do that, send an email from the address of the account to ypodev.official@gmail.com asking for the account to be deleted. We will delete the account and all data synced to it and reply to confirm. Requests from a different address cannot be verified and will not be acted on.
