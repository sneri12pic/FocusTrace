# Preferred usage time — plan

Scope: a daily soft limit per category or per app, set in onboarding and in
Settings > Activity & data. Near and at the limit a brief nudge appears over
a counted app: a soft edge glow and a time label that fade away. Nothing is
blocked.

Non-goals: blocking, notifications, per-weekday limits, cloud sync of limits
beyond the existing settings export.

Acceptance: amber nudge at 90%, red at 100%, repeated on every reopen and
every 5 minutes; the app underneath stays fully usable; a blocked app shows its
block screen and no nudge; limits survive restarts and reboot.

Remaining: confirm the look and touch pass-through on a device.
