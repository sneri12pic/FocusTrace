FocusTrace — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

FocusTrace is a local-first screen-time tracker. The short version: your data stays on your device unless you create an account and turn on cloud sync. Cloud sync is optional and off by default. Tracking, app restrictions and blocking work fully without an account and without a network connection. We do not sell or share your data, and the app contains no analytics or advertising.

Data the app stores on your device

  • App usage (which apps you used, when, and for how long), recorded from Android usage statistics.
  • Your settings: app restrictions you configure, excluded apps, and language preference.

This is stored in a local database inside the app's private storage. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party data processors.

Optional cloud sync

Cloud sync copies your daily usage totals to a FocusTrace account so that another device signed in to the same account can see them. Nothing is sent until you use it:

  • When you create an account or sign in, the app sends your email address and password to the FocusTrace sync server. The server stores the email address and a one-way hash of the password, never the password itself.
  • When you turn on "Sync this device", the app sends, when you press "Sync now" and periodically in the background (about every six hours):
    • a device name (the phone's model name), the platform ("android"), and a random identifier generated by the app for this installation. It is not derived from the hardware and is not an advertising identifier;
    • for each day: the date, your time zone, when that day's totals were last read, and whether they are complete;
    • for each app used that day: the app's package name, its display name, the total time in seconds, and the number of launches.

The server also records when a device was registered and last seen and when each day was received, and it sees your IP address when the app connects, which it uses to limit abusive requests.

Never sent: window titles, per-session or minute-by-minute usage timelines, app icons, notification or screen contents, your restrictions, schedules, limits or other settings, location, contacts, messages, and hardware or advertising identifiers.

Release versions of the app connect to the sync server over HTTPS only. Synced data is kept on the server until you delete your account.

Turning sync off

  • Settings → Account & sync → Sync this device: switching this off stops sending new data. Data already synced stays on the server.
  • Log out also stops sync on this device and keeps the data already synced.
  • To remove the synced data, delete the account (below).

Permissions and why the app needs them

  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — reading screen-time statistics is the app's core function.
  • Query installed apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) — used solely to show app names and icons for the apps you track or restrict, instead of raw package names.
  • Display over other apps — used to show the blocking overlay when an app restriction you configured is reached.
  • Notifications & foreground service — used to keep restriction enforcement running and to show its status.
  • Run at boot — restarts restriction enforcement after your device reboots.
  • Network access (INTERNET) — used only for the optional account and cloud sync. The app makes no other network connections.

Data deletion

On your device: you can erase the local data at any time via Settings → Clear local data inside the app. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Neither of these deletes data already synced to the server.

On the server: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account, confirmed with your password, permanently deletes the account and everything synced to it. Usage history on your device is kept. See Delete your FocusTrace account for details, including what to do if you no longer have the app.

Children

FocusTrace is not directed at children under 13.
